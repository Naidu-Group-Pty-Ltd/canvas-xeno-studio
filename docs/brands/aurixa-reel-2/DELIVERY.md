## Before it runs publicly

- **The music's licence.** _National Treasures_ is Drake's commercial recording. The studio records it as owned by the brand, as confirmed when it was uploaded on 10 October, and Reel 1's handover left its licence for public use to the client to confirm. Before the film is posted, confirm that Aurixa holds the right to use it in a promotional video on each platform it goes to. Without that, a platform may mute it or take the video down. If it can't be cleared, the first master, with the score made for the film, is the version to post.
- **ElevenLabs' plan.** The voiceover is Reel 1's ElevenLabs voice, so the client's ElevenLabs plan must cover commercial use, including ads. So must the score's, if the first master is posted: Eleven Music is cleared for commercial use, including ads, on a paid plan, and film, TV and broadcast need its Enterprise plan. Confirm the account's plan.
- **The voiceover.** The client confirmed the studio's consent statement ("Recorded by or for the brand, with the speaker's permission to use their voice in its content.") when it was imported, and again on 24 October 2026. The studio's rights ledger keeps it as to confirm until the plan is.
- **The free trial.** The film offers a 14-day free trial, in the voice and on the closing card, as Reel 1 does. On 13 October the site's pricing page answered "Is there a free trial?" with a sandbox set up on request. Confirm that the 14-day trial is the offer when it runs (the brand's guardrail 9).
