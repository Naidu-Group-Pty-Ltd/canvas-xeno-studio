# Aurixa Systems · Teaser · _We Are Here_ · delivery

The third teaser, the second version of _As One_, made on 27 October 2026 at the client's brief. It is one continuous
move through the system itself, in the brand's own navy, gold and ice, with the system's light in electric cyan. The
platform's five tiers work as one about its core, and the Aurixa Systems emblem arrives as the system's pulse. It
keeps _As One_'s script word for word and its voice and music ([`PLAN.md`](./PLAN.md) has the brief as it settled).

This is the final: **the fifth version, Aurixa Systems brought to life**, made on 28 October 2026 at the client's
notes on the fourth version ([`PLAN.md`](./PLAN.md), notes 11 and 12). They called the fourth close to perfect, but
missing the power that the first showing of Aurixa Systems should have. The fifth gives it that weight:

- **The light comes on.** The country lies in the night, in a cool moonlight with its towns' lights along the coasts,
  until "operate". Then the system's light runs out from under the core across the whole continent, and the land
  comes up gold behind a front of cyan light, each town flaring as the front passes.
- **The five tiers are boxes of glass**, at the client's note on the tiers. Each comes in on its word about the core
  and shows in motion what it does. Lines of light join each box to its ring and to the boxes beside it, the system's
  pulses run all of them, and the rings keep turning about the core.
- **A living network.** Every link between the forty places carries its own traffic. On "one" a beam of light stands
  up from every place at once, and before the reveal the country's light runs up the threads into the core.
- **The reveal.** The logo arrives in shafts of light, with a long flare across it. A sweep of light crosses it, and
  the dawn comes up under it, the sun's first light a star on the limb.
- **Sound design.** Hits, rises, glass, chimes and shimmers are drawn in code, in the track's key and at its own
  tuning. Xeno Studio's mixer lays them in under the voice, which stays clear over all of it, and the music takes a
  breath before "Aurixa".

The script, the voice and the music are the fourth version's, and so are Australia in relief and the camera's one
journey over it. The fourth version made the system's light electric cyan so that it stands out over the gold land,
and had the soundtrack mixed again by Xeno Studio's own mixer, the music dipped under the voice. The third version
made the film's world. From the first frame to the logo, the world below the system is Australia: a vibrant relief map
of the continent lit from orbit, its ranges in gold and steel, its coast drawn by its own shelf seas against the navy
ocean. The system's threads reach forty places in every state and territory, and those places link up across the
country into one network.

## The picture

One camera, one world, no cut. The world is the emblem's own: navy space and an orb of ice light, and in it the
system's fine lines of light in electric cyan. Below it, from the first frame, lies Australia seen from orbit as a
relief map: its real topography drawn taller than it stands and lit by a key light from the north-north-east with the
shadows its ranges cast; the deserts in the brand's gold, the green in a deep steel, pale ground sand, the salt lakes
white; the shelf seas lifting toward the ice over the navy ocean; every town's lights in gold, turning cyan where the
system reaches them. Until "operate" it lies in the night. The cyan stands out against the gold land wherever the two
meet. Each beat is what the line over it says.

- **The core** (0 to 4.5 s). The whole continent from orbit, about 6,400 km up, at night: the land in a cool moonlight
  at 7% of its day's light, its towns' lights along the coasts. The camera comes down toward it, and on
  "operate" a core of ice light kindles over the country and a thread of light climbs into the sky. The system's light
  runs out from under it across the continent, fast and then slowing, about 3,600 km in 2.6 s: the land comes up gold
  and steel behind a front of cyan light, each town flaring as the front passes, and by 6 s the whole country is lit.
- **The noise** (4.5 to 13.3 s). Panes of software, navy glass lit in electric cyan, crowd the space before the core
  as the voice names them: windows, dashboards, a strip of tabs, the fourth "AI". By "ambition" they have hidden it, and behind them the system has come
  down over the east coast.
- **The question changes** (13.3 to 15.8 s). The panes break, and their glass blows past the lens. On "the question
  changes" the core flares, a wave of light goes out from it, and five faint rings appear about it.
- **The five as one** (15.8 to 20.7 s). One view, the camera circling the core over the Sydney basin, the Blue
  Mountains and the coast, about 540 km up. Each tier lights on its ring as its word is said, and its box comes in about
  the core out of a soft focus: navy glass, frosted over the land behind it, its name in the brand's Inconsolata, and in
  it, in motion, what the tier does.
  - Research is a sweep that finds what it looks for.
  - Clients are a constellation of people joined to one.
  - Reports are pages that gather into one, with its chart and a check.
  - Finance is a line of growth over its bars.
  - Communication is messages going back and forth.

  The five stand as a pentagon with communication at its head. A line of light joins each to its own ring and to the
  boxes beside it, and the system's pulses run all of it. The rings keep their own lives: research's scan, clients'
  constellation, reports' pages, finance's rise, and communication's pulses with the spokes that join every ring to
  every other and to the core. By "communication" all five work at once.
- **Powerful on their own** (20.7 to 22.25 s). The track falls silent. The boxes' lines let go, and the boxes drift
  apart and are gone. The rings drift apart, each on its own tilt, and the system rises over the country. Just before the drop the rings are drawn into the core.
- **The drop** (22.25 s). The core bursts over the country. The five come out of it as one, in one plane and turning
  together, with a flash and a ring of light through the space, and the camera is thrown back and up until the whole
  of Australia lies below, north up the frame, the system over its heart.
- **Separate destinations, one network** (22.95 to 27.7 s). Threads of cyan light fall in a fast cascade from the rings to
  forty places in every state and territory: the eight capitals, Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane, Perth, Adelaide, Hobart,
  Darwin and Canberra, and regional cities from Cairns and Broome to Albany and Launceston. Each answers with a ring
  of light on the ground, and the lights about it turn cyan. As each pair of neighbouring places is reached, the link
  between them runs out across the country, fast: fifty-four links, the east coast's chain, the Hume, Bass Strait to
  Tasmania, the Nullarbor to Perth, the west coast to Darwin, the Stuart and the Barkly up the middle. Pulses race
  along them from one state or territory to another, Perth to Sydney, Darwin to Melbourne, Hobart to Cairns and more,
  at 4,200 km a second, and every link carries its own traffic both ways, points of light running at 900 to 1,600 km
  a second. On "destinations" everything flares; then the Earth comes up to New South Wales.
- **Intelligence, context** (27.7 to 31.8 s). A pulse leaves the rings and runs down a thread to Dubbo, the camera
  beside it, then 269 km along the ground over the Blue Mountains into Sydney, lighting its way. On "context",
  at Banksia Rise, the client's and the property's tags (the brand kit's demo record, Jordan Ellis and 14 Wattle
  Grove) come in beside it and ride with it back up to the system. The network's pulses run on below.
- **As one** (31.8 to 34.25 s). The system rises over the whole country again, and a wave of light crosses it, each
  town and link flaring as it is reached, the pulses racing the network; on "one", on the bar, everything lights at
  once, a beam of light stands up from every one of the forty places, and a ring runs out over the land.
- **The next tool** (34.25 to 36.7 s). Stillness: one pane before the core, the land low in the frame. On "is" it comes
  apart into light that drifts into the system.
- **The system** (36.7 to 40.25 s). The Earth turns east under the system, over inland Queensland. The network's
  traffic stills. The threads are drawn up out of the country, the country's light running up them into the core,
  which gathers it, and the rings stand to face us as the emblem's rings. On "becomes" three bars of light
  draw the emblem's triangle about the core, and on "the system" it locks with a pulse.
- **Aurixa Systems** (40.25 to 45.5 s). The emblem takes the system's place, the core its orb, and a pulse goes out
  from the orb as it lands, with shafts of light from behind it and a long flare across it. The triangle and the rings' fine lines dissipate, and the logo stays as it is. Its
  lettering rises letter by letter, AURIXA and then SYSTEMS on the bar. The light circles inside the A in three rings
  until the orb keeps it, as on _As One_'s end card. On "Systems" and on every bar to the end (40.86, 42.72 and
  44.58 s, the music's own hits) the logo beats: its light swells, its orb flares, a ring goes through the space with
  a fine echo behind it, and the beat runs out over the country below along the network, each town flaring as it
  passes. A sweep of light crosses the logo after "Systems" and again on the last bar. The land runs to the coast and the
  Tasman at the limb, where the dawn comes up under the emblem: a thin gold rim, and the sun's first light, a star with
  its flare.

The logo is the supplied art, placed and never redrawn, in its own gold and ice. The words are the brand's Playfair
Display, with the turn of each line in italic in the system's electric cyan, and the tiers' names, in their boxes, and
the tags are its Inconsolata capitals. No person appears.

**How it was made** (`scripts/aurixa-teaser/we-are-here/README.md`).

- **Nothing in it is generated.** The world is drawn in code (`space.py`), Australia from public map data
  (`earth.py`), and the emblem is the supplied art. The sound design is drawn in code too (`sfx.py`). So it needs no
  "Scenes created with AI" label, and carries none.
- **Australia is map data, drawn as a relief map lit from orbit.** Its shape is Mapzen's terrain tiles (0.3 km a cell
  over the chase's ranges, 0.6 km over Australia, 2.4 km over the region), drawn about five times taller than it
  stands at the chase's height and up to about eighty times from orbit; its light is worked out for every level of
  every grid: the key light on each slope, the shadows the ranges cast, the sky in the valleys. Its colours are NASA's
  Blue Marble carried into the palette, the green laid on the rugged ground close in so the colour follows the relief;
  the shelf seas are the bathymetry's. GLOBE's land mask draws the coast, and GeoNames' places every town's lights and
  the forty places. Each map is read at each pixel's own footprint, so the coast and the relief neither shimmer far
  off nor alias at the limb.
- **Light as a lens records it.** Every point and line is drawn in linear light and high range. The lens has a focus,
  so a near mote is a wide faint disc and a far one a fine point. The air dims what is far, and where the camera moves
  fast a frame is the average of up to eight moments of a half-frame shutter, the towns' lights at as many as their
  motion needs and the land at up to ten, so they streak and blur rather than strobe. The whole is bloomed, given a
  soft shoulder, a lens's slight colour fringe at the edges, a vignette and fine grain. The words are laid over the
  finished picture.
- **The panes** are _As One_'s, redrawn in navy glass and electric cyan, and they break into Voronoi shards that fly
  at the lens.
- **The five boxes** are drawn with the words (`type.py`, `type.js`) and photographed by Chromium, and `comp.py` lays
  frosted glass under each, the picture behind it blurred where the box stands. They are set a little larger than
  drawn, so their names read on a phone.
- **The night and the light.** Before "operate" the land is lit at 7% of its day's light, in a moonlight that
  takes its colour from the sky's ice (`earth.py`), and the light's front runs out from the point under the core on
  the ground itself, pixel by pixel. The reveal's light is drawn over the finished space (`comp.py`): shafts of light
  from behind the logo, held back where the logo stands; its flare; the sweeps of light across it; and the dawn's
  star, hidden where the Earth stands in front of it.
- **The colours.** The system's light is electric cyan (#0FF0FC) with paler cores that go to white where they are
  brightest, and it carries 1.6 times the light the gold did: a dim cyan line reads as teal where a dim gold one read
  as gold. The land, the sky, the core's ice and the logo keep their own colours.

## The sound

_As One_'s soundtrack, mixed again by Xeno Studio's own mixer as the reels' soundtracks are, with the film's own sound
design laid in under the voice (`scripts/aurixa-teaser/we-are-here/soundtrack.ts` and `sfx.py`; the mix's record is
[`soundtrack.json`](./soundtrack.json)). The voice and the music are _As One_'s: Gabby's single dry take of the 95
words, and _National Treasures_ from its first sample at the reference's gains and fade. They are laid out as a studio
edit's audio: the voice on the Editor's Voiceover channel, and the music and the sound design on Music & FX, the sound
design as a sound effect. The studio's own media worker code mixes them as a studio render does: it listens to the
voice for speech, dips the music under it, and brings the mix to the studio's loudness.

- **The sound design gives the film's moments their weight.** It is drawn in code from sine waves and noise (numpy
  alone; nothing is sampled or generated), each sound on its word or bar:
  - a low swell under the night, and on "operate" a deep hit with a shimmer as the light runs out over the country;
  - a glass tick as each pane comes in, and the glass breaking on "ambition";
  - a chime as each box comes in, rising through the key, struck just before its name;
  - a swell drawn in backwards and a rise into the drop, its deep impact, the camera's rush and the network's data;
  - hits on "destinations" and on "one", with a rise into "one";
  - the next tool coming apart, a soft lock as the triangle closes, and the reveal: a swell into "Aurixa", its impact
    and a chord of glass, the light sweeping the logo, a heartbeat under each of its beats, and the sun coming up.

  Every pitched sound is in the track's key, B flat minor, at the track's own tuning. _National Treasures_ is tuned
  about half a semitone sharp of A440 (measured on its stem: the intro's keys 55 cents, the held F under the tiers 43,
  the groove 48), and a sound at A440 would have sat a quarter tone off it.
- **The voice stays clear over it.** Where the voice speaks, the sound design gives way to it in the voice's band
  (250 Hz to 5 kHz), by up to 9 dB, as a dialogue-keyed EQ on an effects bus does. Each hit's first 60 ms passes
  whole, so a hit on a word still lands, and the hits keep their weight low and dry. In the studio's speech band the
  voice stands at least 10 dB over the music and the sound design together in every sentence but "More valuable when
  they stop behaving like separate destinations", which the music alone holds to 9.9 dB (9.8 with the sound design), as
  in the fourth version. The studio's own report, which weighs the voice against the music, reads 12.5 dB through the
  intro, 11.3 through the groove and 17.9 under the reveal, over its 8 dB flag throughout.
- **The hits land.** On the momentary loudness meter, the drop stands 2.9 LU over the fourth version's, and "one"
  2.3 LU.
- **The music takes a breath before "Aurixa".** As "the system." ends, the music falls back 8 dB in 0.2 s, and it is
  whole again on the hit, so that the reveal comes out of a held breath.
- **The music dips under the voice**, as in the fourth version. In _As One_'s mix the voice stood 5 dB over the music
  in the speech band through the intro and 6.1 dB through the groove, under the 8 dB the studio flags as hard to follow
  on a phone's speaker. The music plays at _As One_'s own level wherever the voice is silent, and dips 7.5 dB under it
  in the intro and 6.5 dB in the groove. The sentences come closer together than the studio's 1.2 seconds, so the
  music stays down under all of them rather than pumping between them.
- **The drop's first hit** sounds before the voice comes back, undipped, as it stood in _As One_, now with the sound
  design's impact on it, and settles under the voice in the 0.15 s before the first word. After the last word the
  music comes back up for the last bar's hit as it fades.
- **Every word is where it was**, to the sample at the start of each of the eleven sentences, so every move, light
  and pulse stays on its words and bars (`sound-as-one/cues.json`): "Powerful on their own." and the rise in the
  silent bar, the drop at 22.25 s, "one" on the bar, and the logo's beats on the bars at the end.
- **Its loudness is the studio's own pass**: -14.0 LUFS integrated on FFmpeg's EBU R128 meter (-14.1 as the
  delivered file's AAC decodes; the studio's report, on its loudness filter's own estimate, reads -14.2), true peak
  -1.5 dBTP. The mix's peaks (the voice's) stand 14.6 dB over its loudness, more than one even gain can bring to -14
  LUFS under that peak, so the pass holds those few peaks and rides the gain gently, as a studio render of this mix
  would: within about 1 dB through the film, the first three seconds about 1.2 dB lower, and about 2 dB more for an
  instant on the voice's two loudest words, "So" and "Aurixa".
