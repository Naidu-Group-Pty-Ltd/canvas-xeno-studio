# Aurixa Systems · Teaser · _As One_ · plan

The second teaser, asked for on 27 October 2026 with the client's reference video
([`REFERENCE.md`](../REFERENCE.md)):

- a slightly different concept from what has been made so far, taking what it can from the reference's pictures;
- _National Treasures_ exactly as the reference uses it;
- the brand voice made dry, like the reels;
- the reference's script word for word, with its spacing and pacing adjusted where needed.

It is 45.5 s at 1080 x 1920, like the reference.

## The script

The reference's 95 words, as its voice says them. Nothing is added or left out.

> The next generation of property professionals will operate differently. Not because they'll use more software, not
> because they'll have more dashboards, and not because AI became another tab. At a certain level of ambition, the
> question changes. Research, clients, reports, finance, communication. Powerful on their own. More valuable when they
> stop behaving like separate destinations. When intelligence can move, when context can follow, when the business can
> act as one. So don't ask what the next tool is. Ask what happens when the business becomes the system. Aurixa
> Systems. A new operating standard is coming.

## The concept

The script turns once: from more of everything, in separate places, to one system. _As One_ shows that turn in one place
and with one camera, without a cut. The setting is a hall of black marble in the dark with a single beam of light at its
centre.

- **Act one: the noise.** Software fills the dark around the beam: app windows, then dashboards, then a strip of
  browser tabs that ends with one labelled AI.
- **The turn.** At "At a certain level of ambition", the noise switches off. Every pane turns edge-on and is gone, and
  the beam stands alone.
- **Act two: the five.** Five glass cases stand in a ring around the beam, one for each part of the business the voice
  names. Each case lights as it is named, a hologram of its work inside: research, clients, reports, finance,
  communication. They are powerful on their own, and each is sealed in its own light.
- **Act three: as one.**
  - On the drop, gold light leaps from case to case.
  - The glass lifts away, so the five are no longer separate destinations.
  - A pulse of light moves between them, and the client's context follows it.
  - On "one", on the bar, all five act at once.
- **Act four: the system.**
  - "The next tool" is a sixth case, standing apart and empty, and its light goes out.
  - Then the five draw together into one shape, the emblem's triangle, and it becomes the Aurixa emblem standing over
    the beam: "Aurixa Systems. A new operating standard is coming."

### What it takes from the reference

- the single beam of light rising in fog from black;
- glass display cases on black marble, each holding a hologram;
- gold laser lines between them;
- the serif line with its second half in gold ("At a certain level of ambition, / the question changes.");
- the emblem standing on its pedestal at the end.

The reference shows these in separate generated shots. Here they are one world in one continuous move, and each one does
what the line spoken over it says.

### What makes it different from _Three Questions_

_Three Questions_ fell from orbit onto a city at night, toward one house. _As One_ never leaves one room. Its subject is
not the property but the business, and its motion is things joining, not a camera falling.

It keeps the brand's own type, used the same way:

- every spoken word rises on screen as it is heard, so the teaser reads with the sound off;
- the lines are set in Playfair Display, with the turn of each line in gold italic;
- the labels are in Inconsolata capitals.

The logo is the supplied art, placed and never redrawn. Its lettering rises letter by letter, as in _Three Questions_.

## The sound

- **_National Treasures_, as the reference uses it.**
  - From its first sample, uncut and unstretched, every band at the same gain.
  - -1 dB to the drop; easing from -1.5 to -4 dB by 42 s; faded out from 43.95 s at 12 dB a second, as the reference
    fades it.
  - Nothing is ducked under the voice, as in the reference.
- **The voice.** Gabby, the studio's brand voice, through `generate_voiceover` (`voice.py`).
  - **One take of the whole script.** It reads as one delivery, the way the reference's does, and is cut into its
    sentences in its own silences.
  - **Laid in three runs, each as read.** Only the silences between them are set:
    - sentences 1 to 5 end 0.2 s before the drop, so "Powerful on their own." is heard alone in the bar where the
      track falls silent;
    - sentence 6 begins 0.7 s after the drop;
    - sentences 7 to 11 are placed so that "one" sounds on the bar.
  - **Dry.** No reverb, no compression, no EQ but a 70 Hz high-pass. The reference's voice measures the same: its
    spectrum is within 2 dB of these takes for the same words, and its dynamics match.
  - **Its level.** 4.9 LU over the music through the intro, 2 LU nearer than the reference's.
- **The master.** -14 LUFS integrated, -1 dBTP at most (`scripts/aurixa-teaser/master.py`).

## How it is drawn

Everything is drawn in code in Chromium, frame by frame, at twice the frame's size, with motion blur from drawings spread
over a 180-degree shutter. These are _Three Questions_' renderer and measures (`scripts/aurixa-teaser/`).

- **The world is 3D.** One camera, in metres, projects it.
- **Every flat thing is a plane placed in perspective by a projective transform.** That covers the floor, the panes,
  the glass, the plinths, the tags and the logo.
- **Every line is drawn sharp in the screen's own pixels.** That covers the beam's core, the glass edges, the holograms'
  wireframes and the gold light.
- **The reflections are the same world mirrored in the floor.** They are drawn under the marble, softened and darkened.

The scripts are in `scripts/aurixa-teaser/as-one/`, and their README says what each does.
