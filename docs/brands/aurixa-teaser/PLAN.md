## The idea

The three films tell one purchase: 14 Wattle Grove, Jordan Ellis's next property. Film 1 follows it through the
whole system, Film 2 tells it from the compliance side and Film 3 from the finance side. The teaser puts the three
side by side, in that order.

It opens on the problems the films open on: the client waiting, another identity check, "Can they afford it?" The
brand arrives on the music's drop, as it does in each film. Then each film gets one beat, and each beat is a line
that film already says. Two of them turn on the word "one": Film 2's "One digital passport" and Film 3's "With
just one click". Film 1's is the system itself, "designed around how property professionals actually work." It
closes on two statements, "One property." and "Three films.", and the brand.

Why this way:

- **It claims nothing the films don't.** Every frame and every word is already in an approved film.
- **It sounds and looks like the series.** It has the same voice, the same music and the same signature: the logo
  arriving on the drop, statements on the bass hits, and the living logo at the close.
- **Nothing new to record or license** beyond what the films already need.

The words on screen:

- **Captions.** Every spoken line is captioned word by word, as in the films: silver on a white card before the
  brand arrives (beats 2 to 5), gold on navy glass after it.
- **The film labels.** "FILM 1 · ONE SYSTEM", "FILM 2 · ONE PASSPORT" and "FILM 3 · ONE CLICK" are in the series'
  gold mono, set inside the caption's panel above its line. That keeps them off the picture, and off Film 3's
  illustrative label.
- **The statements.** "One property." and "Three films." are set the way the films set "Your business. Your
  experience. Your decision.": each alone in the frame, its key word in gold italic, on the music's bass hits.
- **The close.** Reel 1's living logo, "The operating system for modern property professionals.", and the release
  line (decision 3) in the gold mono.
- **The cover.** Frame 0 is Australia from orbit with its pins, which is no film's cover. Where a platform takes a
  separate cover, I'd make one from the two statements together.

## The music

_National Treasures_ (instrumental), the films' track, at its own 128.99 BPM:

- **0:00.0–0:09.3 (bars 1 to 5):** the intro's last five bars. It falls silent under the gold hairline, as Films 1
  and 3 do before their drops.
- **0:09.3 (bar 6):** the drop, as the logo leaps out.
- **0:09.3–0:26.0 (bars 6 to 14):** the drop's groove, under the three films.
- **0:26.0–0:29.8 (bars 15 and 16):** the bass hit and the near silence after it, once for each statement, as Films 2 and 3
  set theirs.
- **0:29.8–0:35.0 (bars 17 and 18, then the ring):** the last bar of the track's biggest section, its closing hit at
  0:31.6 as the logo settles, and the cymbal's ring.

The music dips under the voice as in the films, and the mix is measured to -14 LUFS.

## The rules it keeps

- **The product on its demo record.** Every Aurixa screen is the product with Jordan Ellis and 14 Wattle Grove on
  it, and carries DEMO WORKSPACE, as in the films.
- **One modelled figure.** Film 3's capacity counts in and holds for under two seconds, under its illustrative
  label. Nothing the teaser adds covers that label.
- **The Passport, but not its sharing.** The teaser shows the Passport but not one business opening another's, so
  it says nothing about relying on another firm's checks (guardrail 4). "Compliant" appears nowhere.
- **No new claims, numbers or absolutes.** Every word is one the films already say.
- **The supplied logo**, as the films use it.
- **Clear of the platforms.** The top 250 px and bottom 360 px stay clear, and captions sit between 1300 and
  1560 px.

## Decisions

1. **The concept and script as written.** Approve them, or tell me what to change.
2. **The close.** _Recommended:_ end on the brand with a release line. The alternative is the films' trial close
   ("14-DAY FREE TRIAL", "START YOUR FREE TRIAL", pressed on the closing hit). The 14-day trial is still to confirm
   as the offer, and the brand close keeps the teaser clear of it. It also follows the kit's rule that a
   brand-building piece ends on the brand.
3. **The release line.** This depends on how the films go out. If all three post together: "Three films. Watch
   now." If one a week: "Film 1 · [date]". Tell me the plan or the words.
4. **The voice.** _Recommended:_ the films' own lines, as written, with nothing to record. The alternative is to
   speak the teaser's own words as well. You'd record four short lines in the brand voice (Gabby, as for the
   films): "One system.", "One passport.", "One click." and "One property. Three films.". They would play under the
   three film labels and the statements.
5. **A 15-second cut-down.** Optional, once this cut is approved.

## Before it runs publicly

The teaser carries what the films carry:

- **The music licence** for _National Treasures_.
- **ElevenLabs' plan**, which must cover commercial use, for the voice.
- **The compliance review** the films need (Reel 3's decision 12). The teaser shows Film 3's capacity under its
  label and names the Compliance Passport.
- **Two things from Film 1 the same review can settle for all four pieces.** One is the GeoNames credit (CC BY
  4.0) owed for the map's pins. The other is the property photographs, which were generated; the kit asks for
  realistic AI scenes to be labelled.
- **The 14-day trial as the offer,** only if decision 2 takes the trial close.

Film 2's Film gate still holds its _National Treasures_ cut in review. Its picture is the same in both of its cuts,
so the teaser doesn't wait on it.

## How it will be made, once approved

- **The picture.** A teaser renderer (`scripts/aurixa-teaser/`) draws each beat from its film's own scene code at
  final quality, with the film's captions off and the teaser's captions, labels and type on. It is verified by
  agreement, as the films were.
- **The sound.** Each line is cut from its film's clean recording, and _National Treasures_ is cut on its bars.
  The mix is measured as the films were.
- **Two gates** in the studio project _Aurixa Teaser_: the animatic (the whole cut with its sound), then the film.
